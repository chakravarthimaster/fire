The Tag

A short film in slow motion: what a security tag is made of, what happens when someone rips one off a t-shirt, and how the shop finds out.

00:00.0

Inside a hard tag

The clamp

The pin goes through the fabric and into the tag. Inside, small steel balls sit in a cone and grip the pin. A pull only makes the grip tighter. Only the shop's detacher releases it.

The signal

The coil in the tag answers the antennas at the door. If a tag that is still active walks through, the gate sounds an alarm.

Forcing it

Many tags also hold a glass vial of permanent ink. If the tag is broken open, the ink bursts onto the garment, so the shirt is ruined and marked as stolen.
